Manual ١

Allah

The standard

Five on time. Daily istighfar. Idle time is dhikr time. Boundaries respected without debate. Quran contact daily, memorization growing weekly.

The shift

Sulaiman عليه السلام, and behind him Dawud عليه السلام: strength, kingdom, and craft, paired with constant turning back to Allah. The footage is not video, it is the verses: build the scene from the Quran (the wind, the armies, the ant, the throne of Bilqis) and hold the posture it implies: upright, unhurried, certain of Who provides. Use it entering prayer, at temptation, and when success inflates the ego. The line: "This is from the favor of my Lord."

Daily protocol

  • Fajr is the keystone. The system rises and falls with it. Sleep time is set backwards from Fajr, never forwards from the evening.
  • After each prayer: adhkar (33/33/34 + Ayat al-Kursi minimum). Five built-in identity resets a day, stronger than any affirmation.
  • The 30-minute rule: when lost, foggy, or off-track, do not troubleshoot the schedule. Thirty minutes of prayer and Quran. Your own notes recorded it outperforming everything else for clarity. Tested data.
  • Before sleep: Quran audio on, phone otherwise done.

Boundaries

Name your two or three recurring boundary risks privately at the biweekly rewrite. For each, one if-then: "If X situation, I do Y exit." At the moment of temptation, run the Sulaiman shift physically: spine, breath, line. A boundary defended by a body beats one defended by a rule.

Review

The evening check asks one question here: all five on time? If a prayer slips three days running, the schedule around it is wrong. Fix the schedule, not the intention.

Manual ٢

People

The old Social note stays the master tactics reference (reading people, laws, workflows). This manual sits above it: who you become, how you warm up, which body in which room. The Golden Rule is the entire game: get them talking, listen, empathize, respond positively, make them feel good.

The roster

Four bodies plus alternates, each fully specified in the Shifts tab: footage, physicality, line, deployment. Ferguson the Player · Brand the Flow · the Uncle the Anchor · Wayne the Presence, with Khalo Ibrahim and Abo Abdullah as real-footage alternates that often beat celebrity clips because the memory is yours.

With women: the blend

Brand's warmth and verbal ease, at Ferguson's playfulness, governed by Wayne's restraint, carried in the Hit Man's frame. Charming, funny, fully unattached to outcome. The operating rule is subtraction: fewer compliments than you want to give, fewer messages than you want to send, more silence than feels comfortable. Harmless charm is mostly restraint wearing a smile.

Warm-up · before any planned event · 10 min

  • Mouth and voice (3m): tongue twisters aloud, one paragraph read with exaggerated articulation, one minute of free speech. Clear the fog before the room does it for you.
  • Pick the primary shift (1m): party = Ferguson. Networking = Wayne. Strangers or a date = Brand. Depth = Uncle.
  • Run the full shift protocol (2m): use the Run Shift button on the character's card. Stay in the body while getting ready. Walk to the mirror in it.
  • Rehearsal (2m): in the body, first person: door, greeting, first exchange. Then one awkward moment, and you staying loose inside it, Ferguson-style. The awkward rep matters more than the smooth one.
  • Baseline skim (1m): listen actively · question · empathize · compliment · common ground · playfulness.
  • Sharpness token (30s): the one physical trigger, always the same, meaning fully present.

Deployment rules

  • Go first, go positive. You set the temperature of every room you enter.
  • Warm goodbye, every time, even tired. The February regret is the permanent anchor for this rule.
  • One favor asked or given per new connection, when natural.
  • Mid-conversation slip: five-second reset, posture and line only, while they are talking. Nobody sees it.
  • Shifts are worn for people, never against them. Charm in service of making people feel good is the whole license.

The third character: Alone

The old note split the self into Social, Business, Alone, and never defined the one that decides whether the other two are real. The Alone body: the door closes and nothing drops. Same posture, same standards, no audience needed. "Who I am when unseen is who I actually am." If the Alone body holds, the other two stop being performances and become descriptions.

Daily articulation rep

One short video per day, camera on, 60 to 90 seconds on anything, inside the Brand body. Posted or deleted, irrelevant: the rep trains articulation, lens eye contact, and comfort being perceived. From your TikTok note, kept for the right reason: charisma practice, not follower chasing.

Review

After any significant interaction, 60 seconds: which shift was I in, did it hold, what would I redo. Patterns only. Same mistake three times becomes the fortnight's focus.

Manual ٣

Health

The standard

80kg lean at 178cm. From 75kg with some fat: a slow lean bulk, 6 to 12 months of consistency, not a montage.

The shift

David Laid, mid-set. Run it at the gym door: footage, step in, take the body (chest up, walk slower, zero phone), line: "Every rep is filmed, so every rep is perfect." Re-shift between sets if the phone appears. The kitchen has no celebrity; the kitchen runs on four checkmarks.

Nutrition

  • 160g protein daily. Two meals at 50g, two shakes at 30g. The only macro tracked strictly.
  • Calories: ~200 to 300 over maintenance. Scale climbing faster than ~1kg a month = pull back; that speed is mostly fat.
  • No processed junk as default, minimal sugar, gut-friendly rotation. Fixed shopping day, repeatable meal templates, protein never missed by accident.

Training

  • 4 to 5 days a week, calendar-fixed. Example: Upper / Lower / Rest / Push / Pull+Legs / Rest / conditioning.
  • Progressive overload on compounds: squat or leg press, hinge, bench or dips, rows, overhead press, pull-ups.
  • Abs 3x weekly: hanging raises, cable crunches, planks. Abs appear at low body fat plus load.
  • Cardio 2 to 3x weekly, 20 to 30 minutes, easy enough not to eat recovery. Boxing counts.

Sleep

Fixed wake anchored to Fajr, sleep set backwards, 7.5 to 8 hours. Phone out of reach, Quran on, dark and cool. The under-eye issue is a sleep and screen problem before it is a serum problem.

Supplements

  • Take: creatine monohydrate 5g daily · omega-3 (1 to 2g EPA/DHA) · vitamin D3 if low · magnesium glycinate evenings if sleep needs help · whey for the two shakes.
  • Optional: caffeine pre-training, electrolytes.
  • Peptides: not without a physician and bloodwork. Gray-market, mostly injectable, unregulated purity. Sleep, protein, creatine, and consistency deliver 95% of what the marketing promises.
  • NAD-adjacent: thin evidence for young healthy men. Spend it on better food.

Review

Weekly, three numbers in the Review tab: bodyweight average, sessions done, protein days. Monthly: progress photo, same lighting, same pose.

Manual ٤

Looks

The shifts

In the bathroom: the Routine (American Psycho's discipline, the man discarded). Precise, unrushed, every step complete. "There is an exact way this is done, and I do it exactly." Exists to kill the skipped-step tired-tonight version.

Out of the bathroom: Jordan Barrett. Effortless, unbothered, never checking himself. "It is handled, so I never think about it again." Exists to kill vanity-checking. The routine is obsessive so the day does not have to be.

AM · ~10 min · fixed order

  • Face wash · vitamin C serum · moisturizer · SPF, daily (the single highest-return skin habit, beats every corrective).
  • Under-eye caffeine serum if using; sleep does more.
  • Hair styled properly. Teeth brushed; whitening strips on the product's cadence, not daily forever.
  • Deodorant, then fragrance on moisturized skin. Contacts and light corrective makeup when the day calls: enhancement, not mask.

PM · ~5 min

  • Cleanse · treatment (retinoid or scar active, introduced slowly) · moisturizer · lip balm.

Weekly slot · fixed day · 30 min

Beard line-up and trim, nails, brows, hair maintenance, wardrobe check for the week: ironed, planned, occasion-matched.

Occasion prep · dates, events, hosting

Full shower and hygiene sequence · mouth complete (scrape, floss, paste, balm, gum on hand) · lotion then perfume · hair styled · contacts · outfit planned · jewelry · nails checked · light makeup if using. Hosting: space cleared, ambient light, scent, coffee and dates per your hospitality standard. Run it in the Routine body; close the mirror and shift to Barrett for the night.

Standing rules

  • Barber every 2 to 3 weeks, booked in advance.
  • Fit beats brand, ironed beats new, one style identity beats ten trends. Dress the character, not the algorithm.
  • Grooming TikTok gets a bounded 20-minute slot, saved to a list, acted on at the weekly slot.
Manual ٥

Career

The shift

Feynman. Run it at the start of every deep block: the grin, the chalk, the delight. "I want to actually understand this, not sound like I do." Deploy also on hard papers (confusion is the fun part) and whenever explaining anything, because explaining like him is the profession you are heading toward. Wearing him now is rehearsal for the actual job.

The daily deep block

  • 90 to 120 minutes, every day, before shallow work. Phone in another room. This block is the career.
  • Content rotates: course material, textbooks, implementation, papers, research writing.
  • The spine: quantum decoherence, information geometry, ML-for-quantum. Every block traces to it or a named prerequisite.

Weekly structure

  • One paper or serious source weekly, summarized in one page, own words. You do not need more input, you need output.
  • One building session weekly minimum: code, experiment, or write.
  • World literacy, 30 min twice a week: markets, tech, Gulf and global politics. Enough to speak intelligently, not enough to become a news consumer.

Ventures

  • Venture shift: Hormozi. Forward, blunt, calm. "Find the real constraint, do the boring work, ignore everything else." Feynman is for understanding; Hormozi is for shipping.
  • Fixed, bounded block on set days. Never the deep block's slot. Study is scheduled first; ventures fill remaining capacity, never the reverse.
  • One active venture gets real effort. Others park, reviewed monthly.

Review

Weekly: deep blocks out of 7, summary done, building session done. Below 5 of 7 for two straight weeks = something structural is stealing them; find it at the rewrite.

Life

You, from the camera roll. Before booking anything and whenever motivation dips: Japan, Thailand, the bike, the range. Watch it as footage, step into the version of you living it. "This is what the money and discipline are for." Your own note said the camera roll should be enough motivation. Formalized here.

The active roster · three at a time

Anime and entertainment

Scheduled, therefore guilt-free: the evening wind-down slot after the day's checkmarks. A reward with a container, not a leak. If it appears outside its container, something upstream (sleep, stress, avoidance) needs looking at.

Travel and experiences

  • One per month minimum: trip, new city, new activity, new venue. Booked in the monthly review, before the month starts.
  • Build each around people or a skill. Your own travel notes prove it: connection or challenge fed you; pure consumption left nothing.
